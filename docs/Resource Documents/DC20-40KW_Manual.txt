Act 3 Product function characteristics
3.1 Main function
1）Input protection
With input over and under voltage, three-phase imbalance, lack of equal protection
function.
2）Output protection
With the output over and under voltage, over current, over temperature, overload, short
circuit, insulation detection, battery reverse connection, battery current backflow
and other protection functions
3）Remote control
When an exception occurs, remote start and stop control and remote upgrade can be
implemented through the background
4）System exception protection
During the charging process, in case of emergency (starting the emergency stop switch,
communication failure with the battery management system, control guidance, input
power failure, etc.), the charging function will disconnect the DC output contactor
within 100ms, and the output voltage will drop to less than 60V within 1s.
5）Overtemperature protection
① If the air filter is blocked, the cooling fan fails, or the internal temperature
of the charging pile exceeds the overtemperature protection value, the charging
pile reduces the output power or cuts off the DC output, and an alarm is generated.
② When the temperature of the DC charging port exceeds the overtemperature protection
value (temperature sensor on the charging gun), the charging pile reduces the output
power or cuts off the DC output, and generates an alarm.
③ When the battery temperature exceeds the overtemperature protection value, the BMS
demand will be reduced, and the charging pile will automatically reduce the output
power according to the demand, so as to protect the battery and extend the service
life of the battery.
6）Insulation detection function
The charging pile is provided with an insulation detection circuit. After the vehicle
interface is connected and before the vehicle charging loop contactor is closed, the
charging pile closes the DC output loop contactor to test the internal insulation
(including the charging cable). If the insulation performance is abnormal, the
charging will not start.
